Level 2 Multi-fidelity RL Results

1. Scenario description:
In model-based RL like Dyna-PPO, early performance can be deceptive.
At low fidelity budgets (500 steps), the world model is untrained and inaccurate.
Setups with high imag_horizon use bad model data early on and get low scores,
making them look bad to multi-fidelity pruners.

2. Optimization history and analysis:
The late bloomer configuration was eliminated in round 1 (budget 500 steps).
The early short-term signals tricked the optimizer.
Optimizer selected configuration: {'imag_horizon': 2, 'imag_batches': 3, 'model_epochs': 2} (Score: 27.95)
Early eliminated late bloomer:    {'imag_horizon': 12, 'imag_batches': 12, 'model_epochs': 5} (Score: 31.42)

3. Conclusion:
Yes, multi-fidelity optimization was negatively affected.
An excellent final hyperparameter setup was dropped too early
because early metrics did not correlate with final performance.

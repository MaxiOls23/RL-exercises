1. How does the length of trajectories affect training?
Longer trajectories increase the variance of return estimates, making gradients noisy and training unstable.
They also worsen the temporal credit assignment problem and delay updates.

2. Does the same network architecture and learning rate work for LunarLander-v2?
No. First of all I couldn't test it on LunarLander-v2, because I got a deprecated error.
Second the avg return is much worse with -719 which indicates that the network is not working well with LunarLander.

3. How is the sample complexity related to the DQN?
Reinforce has much worse (higher) sample complexity than DQN.
Reinforce is on-policy and discards data after a single update, 
while DQN is off-policy and efficiently reuses data via an experience replay buffer.

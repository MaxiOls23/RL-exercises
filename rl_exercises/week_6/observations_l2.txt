PPO vs. Actor-Critic:
- Both PPO versions perform much better than the standard Actor-Critic baseline.
- The standard Actor-Critic is very unstable. It learns slowly and has huge drops in performance, with scores falling below -1500.
- This shows that standard policy methods struggle because they make updates that are too large. PPO's clipping mechanism fixes this by keeping policy changes small and safe.

Vanilla PPO vs. Enhanced PPO:
- Vanilla PPO and Enhanced PPO perform almost exactly the same. Both quickly find a good, stable strategy after about 40,000 steps.
- This happens because LunarLander-v3 is a relatively simple environment. The core parts of PPO (clipping and mini-batch training) are already enough to solve it completely.
- Extra features like learning-rate annealing or global gradient clipping do not help much here because the default settings are already highly optimized.

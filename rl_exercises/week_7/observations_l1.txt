1. Is RND a good fit for DQN and PPO?
- PPO + RND: Yes, it is a great fit. PPO learns from current data and
  throws it away immediately. This means the exploration rewards are
  always fresh and correct.
- DQN + RND: No, it is a bad fit. DQN saves old data in a
  replay buffer. By the time DQN uses that data to learn, the exploration
  rewards are old and wrong.

2. Is there a way to see the reason from your training runs? (lunarlander_exploration_comparison.png)
- The green line climbs the fastest and reaches the highest score.
  This shows that because PPO uses fresh data immediately,
  it can instantly turn the exploration rewards into progress.
- DQN + RND (blue line) stays under PPO + RND suggesting worse performance
- Epislion Greedy (orange line) is clearly the worst from all strategies which indicates the low scores.
